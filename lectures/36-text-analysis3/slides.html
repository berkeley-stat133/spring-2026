We introduced the core ideas of Sentiment Analysis:

Consider the text as a combination of its individual words or tokens, and the sentiment of the whole text as the sum of the sentiment content of the individual words.

Sentiment lexicons

  • "bing": from Bing Liu; this lexicon1 categorizes words in a binary fashion into positive and negative categories.

  • "afinn": from Finn Arup Nielsen; this lexicon assigns words with a score that runs between -5 and 5, with negative scores indicating negative sentiment and positive scores indicating positive sentiment.

  • "nrc": from Saif Mohammad and Peter Turney; this lexicon categorizes words in a binary fashion ("yes" / "no") into categories of positive, negative, anger, anticipation, disgust, fear, joy, sadness, surprise, and trust.

  • "loughran": another lexicon somewhat similar to "afinn".
